Description

Short. Veilkeeper is a tactical RPG about the cost of pushing too far. You lead expeditions into unstable rifts, and every relay you leave behind to hold the line is a soldier who does not fight in the next battle. The army that reaches the end is a fraction of the one that entered.

Long. Veilkeeper is built around the tension between presence and absence. You play the Veilkeeper, a figure with no memory of the world’s history, rediscovering it through play. You never enter combat yourself - you remain on the home shard maintaining the rift connection that makes expeditions possible, and your influence reaches the battlefield through the heroes you send.

Expeditions push into rifts along a chain of signal relays, and holding that chain costs bodies. Units left behind to garrison a relay are units that do not reach the next fight, so the force thins as the objective gets closer. Deciding which unit to leave where is as consequential as any individual battle.

Between expeditions, the home shard is designed to keep running without you. A planned stream integration layer will let viewers inhabit that world while you are away. Veilkeeper is designed to remain fully playable offline; stream integration is an optional enhancement rather than a requirement.

What is playable today

The tactical combat layer runs end to end: draft a squad, deploy it, fight, win or lose. It is the only layer that currently exists as software, and it is still under active development. Combat and units in detail: veilkeepergame.com/gameplay

  • Grid tactics - turn-based combat with movement, facing, and positional damage from side and rear attacks.
  • Four archetypes - Knight, Archer, Assassin and Mauler, separated by stat profile, reach and how their weapon interacts with armor rather than by abilities.
  • Readable resolution - damage type and delivery resolve against armor type through a published table. Every attack shows its damage before you commit to it.
  • Full input support - mouse, keyboard and controller are all first-class.

The demo run. The press build shown at Play NYC 2026 is a scripted three-fight run of about five minutes. You draft six units from a pool of twelve, then choose exactly one unit to leave behind between each fight. Your force shrinks from six to five to four while the opposition grows from three to four to five.

The roadmap

Veilkeeper is built outward from combat. Each layer has to stand on its own before the next leans on it.
